Chatbot & Data Privacy Notice

The chat assistant on this site has been reviewed for its intended purpose: answering common questions about our services, pricing, hours, and how to book an appointment.

What it collects

Only the messages you choose to type into the chat window, so it can respond to you.

What it does not collect

It does not access your AutoMan account, password, appointment history, or payment details. Chatting is optional and separate from logging in.

How your message is handled

This chat widget is provided by a third-party assistant service (Tuqlas). When you send a message, it goes directly to that service to generate a reply — AutoMan's own servers do not receive or store the contents of your chat.

Is it stored?

Not by AutoMan. The chat provider may keep a record of the conversation to operate the service, under its own privacy practices, separately from AutoMan's systems.

Shared with third parties?

AutoMan does not sell or share chat content, since we don't receive it in the first place — the conversation happens directly between you and the chat provider.

Purpose

Purely to help you get quick answers without waiting for staff. For anything account-specific — bookings, quotations, payments — please use your logged-in dashboard or contact us directly at (63) 968-236-7189.
